# TerraEcho Data Sources

TerraEcho combines historical environmental datasets from reputable scientific and governmental sources to create a unified timeline (1900–2035) for storytelling and ML forecasting.

## CO₂ — Modern Atmospheric Measurements
File:
co2_modern_mauna_loa.csv

Source:
NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory / Scripps Mauna Loa Observatory

Description:
Annual mean atmospheric CO₂ concentrations from Mauna Loa observations.

---

## CO₂ — Historical Reconstruction
File:
co2_historical_reconstruction.txt

Source:
Etheridge et al. Antarctic Ice Core Reconstruction
NOAA ESRL historical CO₂ compilations

Description:
Pre-1958 reconstructed atmospheric CO₂ values derived from Antarctic ice-core measurements and adjusted for global mean estimates. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}
